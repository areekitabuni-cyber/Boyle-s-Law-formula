Boyle’s Law

For a fixed amount of gas at constant temperature, pressure and volume are inversely proportional.

Simple meaning:
When pressure increases, volume decreases. When pressure decreases, volume increases.

Why? If the piston compresses the gas, the molecules have less space and hit the cylinder walls more frequently. At constant temperature, this increases pressure.
